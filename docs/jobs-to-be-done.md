# Jobs To Be Done

### Job J-1 (Belongs to Role: Solo Freelancer)
When I am drafting a quote for a prospective client, I want to accurately determine my minimum billable project rate based on my overhead costs and tax obligations, so I can ensure the engagement remains profitable and sustainable for my business.

### Job J-2 (Belongs to Role: Solo Freelancer)
When scope adjustments or revisions arise during a project, I want to recalculate the financial impact of added hours and expenses, so I can communicate revised pricing with confidence and avoid uncompensated scope creep.

### Job J-3 (Belongs to Role: Small Agency Account Lead)
When pitching a multi-resource proposal to a budget-conscious client, I want to evaluate various markup percentages and volume discounts against direct contractor costs, so I can present a competitive quote while protecting agency margins.
