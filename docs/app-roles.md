# App Roles

### Role R-1: Solo Freelancer
A Solo Freelancer is an independent contractor or creative professional who bills clients on hourly, day, or milestone bases and needs to determine their take-home income and billable rates. They can enter estimated project hours, billable rates, expected business overhead expenses, and local tax rates to calculate client quotes, net earnings, and effective take-home pay. They must never see negative take-home earnings without a prominent warning, nor be permitted to calculate fees using zero or negative billable hours.

### Role R-2: Small Agency Account Lead
A Small Agency Account Lead is a client manager who prepares client proposals involving subcontractor day rates, agency margins/markups, and client discounts. They can toggle between hourly and flat-fee project modes, specify agency profit margin percentages, add contingency buffers, and generate transparent line-item fee breakdowns. They must never apply a discount greater than 100% or produce an invoice fee that undercuts direct subcontractor pass-through costs.
